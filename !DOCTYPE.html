01

Introduction

Is Vitamin D deficiency the missing link underlying diminished emotional intelligence and exaggerated stress reactivity?
Vitamin D as a Neuroactive Steroid & Neuro-Regulator Vitamin D expresses Vitamin D Receptors (VDRs: PDIA3, NR1I1) via genomic and non-genomic pathways across the prefrontal cortex, hippocampus, and amygdala, modulating serotonin and dopamine synthesis vital for affect regulation and stress adaptation.
Trait Emotional Intelligence (EI) & The "Emotional Recession" Trait EI encompasses emotional self-awareness, expression, empathy, and regulation. With rising global concerns over an "emotional recession" driving medical trainee burnout and disengagement, identifying modifiable biological correlates of EI is critical.
Cardio-Adrenal Autonomic Stress Reactivity (CAAR) Dynamic physiological magnitude of cardiovascular (blood pressure and heart rate) and adrenocortical (serum cortisol) shifts elicited by acute psychological or physical stressors. Serving as an essential objective index of stress vulnerability, CAAR bridges autonomic nervous system and hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis activation during high-pressure challenges.
OBJECTIVES: This integrated study aimed to investigate the associations of Vit D 25(OH)D3 with trait EI and cardiovascular and adrenocortical responses to acute stress i.e., CAAR in undergraduate medical students.

Limitations & Future Implications

Study Limitations

  • Cross-Sectional Architecture: Precludes directional causal inference; temporal sequencing between 25(OH)D₃ repletion and stress reactivity cannot be definitively proven without prospective intervention.
  • Cohort Specificity: Single-center sample of young adult medical students (18–25 yrs, normal Asian BMI, male predominance 71.8%) warrants replication in broader clinical populations.
  • Psychometric Measure: Self-report SSEIT captures perceived Trait EI rather than maximum-performance Ability EI (e.g., MSCEIT).
  • Unmeasured Covariates: Dietary calcium/magnesium intake and continuous 24-hr Holter ECG heart rate variability (HRV) metrics were not logged.

Future Directions & Clinical Implications

  • Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs): Double-blind, placebo-controlled cholecalciferol intervention trials to evaluate whether biochemical correction directly rescues Trait EI and dampens CAAR.
  • Cardiovascular Deep-Phenotyping: Integrating continuous beat-to-beat baroreflex sensitivity (BRS), respiratory sinus arrhythmia (RSA), and inflammatory cytokine profiling (IL-6, TNF-α).
  • Curricular Medical Policy: Implementation of the "Dual-Lens Strategy"—routine 25(OH)D₃ screening paired with accredited emotional resilience training to mitigate burnout in medical trainees.
